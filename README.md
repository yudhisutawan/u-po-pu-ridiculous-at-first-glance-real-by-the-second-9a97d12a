Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a):
one sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a bizarre startup idea that
sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

## The entry

> We sell 3,000-year-old honey excavated from Egyptian tombs to wealthy
> foodies who pay $500 a spoonful for the oldest snack on Earth.

132 characters. The winner of a 6-candidate head-to-head tournament judged
against the bounty brief — see [TOURNAMENT.md](TOURNAMENT.md). The pitch
turns on a real fact: honey found in Egyptian tombs is still edible after
~3,000 years, and rare-food collectors pay absurd prices for antiquity.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
Standard library only.
